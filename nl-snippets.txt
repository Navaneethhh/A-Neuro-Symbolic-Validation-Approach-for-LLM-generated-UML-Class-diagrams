In a hospital network, patients make medical appointments with assigned doctors. The system tracks patient details like full name, contact, and medical summary. Appointments record the date, time, and current status. Doctors can issue prescriptions containing medication details and dosages. Each appointment produces a billing record with total cost.
A medical clinic manages staff members. Physicians and nurses are types of staff. Physicians handle patient consultations, write diagnostic reports, and record health observations. Nurses assist physicians and perform vital checks. The system connects health observations directly to patient medical records and department reports.
An online shopping platform maintains registered customers, shopping carts, and products. Customers place orders that contain multiple order items. Shopping carts calculate totals and handle item additions or removals. Orders generate payments that have amount and transaction status.
An inventory system manages warehouses, suppliers, and stock items. Warehouses store stock items supplied by various suppliers. Suppliers provide shipments with delivery dates and tracking numbers. Warehouse managers oversee warehouse operations and initiate restock orders when items drop below threshold.
A university library tracks book titles, physical copies, and library members. Members borrow physical copies according to checkout rules. Books can be prerequisites or recommendations for other books. Librarians approve loan extensions and calculate overdue fines for late returns.
A digital media library allows patrons to reserve digital media items like eBooks and audiobooks. Media items have title, publisher, and availability state. Patrons can renew loans, stream media content, and pay late fees. System administrators maintain patron accounts and update media catalog entries.
